Design Leadership · 20 Years in the Room

HOW I
LEAD.

01

I lead by feeling the friction first

+

Before a single decision gets made, I need to understand what it feels like to use the broken thing every day. Not the stated problem, the actual experience.

02

I lead by building the pattern, not the solution

+

A great solution to one problem is a good week. A pattern that solves the next ten without me in the room, that's what I'm actually building toward.

Proof: the RealCrowd pattern library and dev code style guide I created, coded and managed. The shared Help Center system at Meta. Enterprise patterns at Salesforce still in production.

03

I lead by still holding the pen

+

I run teams and I still sketch the first direction myself. My team deserves a leader who can show them what great looks like, not just describe it.

Proof: I coded part of the Data Import Wizard prototype myself before engineering built it.

04

I lead by making the bet visible

+

Every design decision is a bet on what the user values. I surface that bet so the team debates the right thing, not the pixels, but the assumption underneath them.

05

I lead by deciding what the product will not do

+

At Meetamore, the relationship app I co-founded, the hardest design work was what the AI would refuse to say and what it would never remember. I hired a team of 12 and ran it across three continents, and we held 95% on-time delivery because those boundaries were settled before the screens were.

Proof: Meetamore, on iOS, Android and the web
